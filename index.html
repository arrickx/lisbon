Tips & Survival Guide

Essential local wisdom & practical Portuguese.

👟

Calçada Portuguesa Warning

Lisbon’s beautiful mosaic pavements are extremely slick, especially when wet or polished by footsteps. Wear grippy sneakers; avoid smooth soles or heels!

🎫

Save on Transit (Zapping)

Never pay cash onboard Tram 28 (€3.10). Buy a reusable Navegante Occasional card (€0.50) at any metro station and load it with Zapping for ~€1.60 per ride.

🥖

The "Couvert" in Restaurants

Bread, olives, and cheese brought to your table before the meal are not free. If you eat them, you will be charged. If you don't want them, simply leave them untouched.

☕

Order Coffee Like a Local

Ask for a "Uma bica" — the traditional Lisbon term for a rich espresso with thick crema. Pair it with a warm pastel de nata sprinkled with cinnamon!
